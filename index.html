How to record a capture
  1. Open chrome://net-export in Chrome or edge://net-export in Edge.
  2. Keep the default privacy mode. Start logging, reproduce the issue in another tab, then stop logging.
  3. Choose the saved JSON for A. To compare, repeat the same action under the second condition and load it as B. Short, similar runs make the counts easier to interpret.
